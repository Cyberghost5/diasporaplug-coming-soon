Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Effective for DiasporaPlug EU Pre-Launch Operations

1. Overview & Data Controller

DiasporaPlug EU ("DiasporaPlug", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the personal data and privacy of our community across the European Union. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, protect, and process personal information when you sign up for our early-access waitlist, apply as an artisan, or interact with our platform.

Data Controller contact: privacy@diasporaplug.eu

2. Information We Collect

  • Waitlist Participants: Your email address, chosen EU metropolitan city (e.g. Paris, Berlin, Amsterdam, Brussels), date/time of registration, and referral traffic data.
  • Artisan Applicants: Full name, craft/trade classification, operating city, phone/WhatsApp contact information, and portfolio verification materials.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized telemetry including browser type, language preferences, and device characteristics strictly necessary to deliver a responsive, 60fps user experience.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): When you voluntarily submit your email to receive early access updates, launch invitations, and founding member promotions.
  • Pre-Contractual Steps (Art. 6(1)(b)): When artisans apply to join our verified provider network.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Protecting platform integrity, preventing bot abuse, and maintaining fair waitlist positions.

4. Zero Selling & Data Security

We enforce a strict Zero Data Selling Guarantee. We will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your email address or personal details to third-party advertisers or data brokers. All transmissions are encrypted in transit via modern TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. Your Rights Under EU GDPR

As an individual in the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess full statutory rights:

Right of Access Request a complete copy of the data we hold on you.
Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten") Request immediate deletion of your email from our waitlist.
Right to Rectification Update or correct your registered details or preferred city.
Right to Withdraw Consent Unsubscribe instantly with one click from any launch email.

6. Contact Our Privacy Team

To exercise any of your GDPR rights, request data deletion, or ask questions regarding our privacy architecture, please contact:

privacy@diasporaplug.eu
